# Snake-Game
# 🐍 Pro Snake Game

Advanced **Snake Game** built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.  
Designed for **smooth gameplay**, **mobile-friendly controls**, and **fun arcade experience**.

---

## 🎮 Features

- Classic Snake gameplay with **grid-based movement**  
- **Keyboard controls** (Arrow keys)  
- **On-screen buttons** for mobile-friendly play  
- **Coins (Food)**: Eat to grow snake and increase score  
- **Gradual speed increase** as you collect more coins  
- **Start & Reset buttons** for controlled gameplay  
- **High replay value** with growing snake and speed challenge  
- Clean and structured code, easy to modify for learning  

---

## 🕹 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your browser  
2. Use **Arrow Keys** or **on-screen buttons** to move the snake:  
   - ⬆️ Up  
   - ⬇️ Down  
   - ⬅️ Left  
   - ➡️ Right  
3. Eat the gold coins to grow your snake and increase your score  
4. Avoid walls and your own body  
5. Reset button starts a fresh game  

---

## ⚡ Technology Stack

- **HTML5 Canvas** – For smooth rendering  
- **CSS3** – Responsive styling & button design  
- **JavaScript** – Game logic, movement, scoring, and collision detection
